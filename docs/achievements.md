# Levels and achievements: full notes

Moved verbatim from CLAUDE.md on 2026-11-09 to keep CLAUDE.md under its size limit. Every achievement's design, names, tier changes and the user's calls. The code (`server/src/services/gamification.js`, `achievementStats.js`) is the authority on current behaviour; this file records why. Read it before adding, renaming or rebalancing an achievement.

**Gamification** (`services/gamification.js`): `LEVELS` array and `ACHIEVEMENTS` array are the authoritative definitions for all level thresholds and achievement logic. **Levels (2026-11-02):** 100 levels; 1-60 kept their XP thresholds, 61-100 climb from 40,000 to 100,000 (each step a little longer, about 1,200 to 1,800). All 100 titles were rewritten as one path through Prague's history (newcomer and street trades, guilds, scholars with Kepler's Assistant at 28, town offices, knights and nobles, the kingdom's high offices to King of Bohemia at 60, emperors, legends, the city's keepers, Soul of Prague at 100); level numbers didn't change, only titles. **Achievements pay XP** (`ACHIEVEMENT_XP`: easy 25, medium 150, hard 400, extreme 800, user's numbers): at a check-in it is added before the level is worked out; on a sync it is `$inc`ed in the same guarded update as the `$push`, and `/auth/me` and `GET /api/user/achievements` return `achievementProgress` ({ levelBefore, levelInfo, totalXP }) so the client can open the level-up modal (AuthContext). Difficulty is shown as tiers: bronze (easy), silver (medium), gold (hard), diamond (extreme), on the detail badges, the solid XP badge and the frame of unlocked icons (`--diff-*` in `achievements.css`). Since 2026-11-02 the tiers are also the displayed names (Bronze/Silver/Gold/Diamond, Bronzová/Stříbrná/Zlatá/Diamantová, 青铜/白银/黄金/钻石, user's call); the stored keys stay easy/medium/hard/extreme. `evaluateAchievements` runs on every check-in, and since 2026-11-02 (user's call: unlock as soon as the conditions are met) also via `syncAchievements` (`services/achievementStats.js`) on `GET /api/auth/me` (app load) and `GET /api/user/achievements`.
