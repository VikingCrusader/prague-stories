# Area guides: full notes

Moved verbatim from CLAUDE.md on 2026-11-09 to keep CLAUDE.md under its size limit. Read it before writing or changing an area guide, or the map and pills that open them.

**Area guides (2026-11-04, user's idea):** a longer write-up per správní obvod and, later, per katastrální území, in `utils/areaGuides/{en,cz,zh}.js` (one lazy chunk per language; German falls back to English), shown by `AreaGuideModal.jsx` with the user's progress there (collected / total cards, guests see the total). Opened from the pills in `DistrictLine`, the profile's district map (`DistrictMap.jsx`: on the user's own profile, clicking an already-highlighted district, i.e. one click with a mouse or a second tap on a phone, opens its guide; it used to open Explore; a toggle above it switches to a quarter view of all 112 katastrální území (no district borders drawn on top since 2026-11-08, user's call: too busy), coloured from `quarterProgress`, which `GET /api/user/progress` now returns beside `districtProgress`; the district view is the default; only the quarter view zooms, by mouse wheel, drag to pan, two-finger pinch and +/−/reset buttons, moving the SVG viewBox, with `vector-effect: non-scaling-stroke` so borders stay thin; unzoomed it is `touch-action: pan-y` so a finger still scrolls the page, and a drag never counts as a click; from 2x zoom (`LABEL_ZOOM`) quarter names appear at a fixed on-screen size, each only if its quarter is wider on screen than the name, so more names show the further you zoom; the toggle also switches the card's title, summary and list below the map (`DistrictProgress.jsx`) to quarters: every quarter with cards, grouped by district and sorted by name, 21 a page with previous / next; the card must stay as tall as before the toggle existed (user's call): the toggle sits in the title line (`DistrictMap` gets `showToggle={false}`), and the pager is a one-row item in the list's own grid, so 21 quarters plus the pager take the same 8 rows of three as the 22 districts; the last page is padded with empty rows; on the user's own profile every row, district or quarter, opens the area's guide like the map (2026-11-08; rows used to go to Explore)), the "More about this area ›" link after the Explore area blurb, and the Explore "where you are" badge, whose district and quarter are separate buttons (user's call, 2026-11-04). Every area opens, guide or not: one without a guide yet shows its name and progress and "内容还在开发中" (`areaGuide.comingSoon`). **Split of content (user's call):** district guides are about life today (sections 概览 / 刻板印象 / 日常 / 趣闻 / 怎么逛: who lives there, Praguers' stereotypes; the stereotypes come straight after the overview as the hook, and 日常 then answers or bears them out, so a stereotype should only lean on what 概览 has said (order changed 2026-11-04, user's call); schools and work are the last paragraphs of 日常, merged 2026-11-04, and named in 概览 too when they define the district, like Prague 6's universities), with little history; history goes in the quarter guides (概览 / 历史 / 人文 / 趣闻 / 最著名的地标 / 怎么逛), and the two shouldn't repeat. **Quarters come in three tiers (user's plan, 2026-11-04):** the historic towns and big neighbourhoods (Old Town, Lesser Town, Hradčany, Josefov, New Town, Vyšehrad, Vinohrady, Žižkov, Smíchov, Karlín, ...) in full; about 40 old suburbs and big villages in 3-4 sections of 400-600 ZH characters; the small villages in two short sections (概览, 历史) plus one quirk. Give each village its own hook (the name's meaning, one person or event, today's contrast) instead of the shared first-mention / monastery / Thirty Years' War / joined Prague in 1922-1974 template. The landmarks section is `{ h, landmarks: true }` in the data files; its cards are listed once by slug in `AREA_LANDMARKS` (`utils/areaGuides/index.js`), shown as name tags read from the cards (collected ones gold with a tick), so names need no translation. Teasers in 趣闻 / 怎么逛 should point at details not on that list. **Missing cards (user's request, 2026-11-04):** while picking a guide's landmarks, check the live DB; a well-known place with no card goes into `docs/missing-landmarks.md`, and the user is told about new entries at the end of each batch (most useful for the outer quarters). **No stereotypes section in quarter guides (user's call, 2026-11-04):** stereotypes stay in the district guides (most of them are really about one quarter, so repeating them would show the same joke twice); a quarter's 人文 may instead tell where its reputation came from (Žižkov's working-class pubs and politics, why Bohnice has its hospital, Karlín as Prague's first suburb), only where there is one. Name few specific places and leave them for the player to find. Opening a guide that has text (not a coming-soon one) while signed in is recorded on `User.areaGuidesRead` (`POST /api/user/area-guide-read`, keys like `district:Praha 7`); ten different areas unlock `know_the_area` (门儿清 / Know Your Way Around / Vyznám se tu, Bronze, 2026-11-04, user's idea, about getting to know the areas, with the tram ride as the joke; the sync only runs when it can fire, like Turning the Page; icon `know_the_area.webp`, prompt in `docs/achievement-icons.md`). All 22 districts written (7-22 on 2026-11-04, the outer ones short where there is little to say); quarters so far: Staré Město, Malá Strana, Nové Město, Josefov, Hradčany, Vyšehrad, Vinohrady, Žižkov, Smíchov, Karlín, Holešovice, Libeň, Bubeneč, Dejvice, Břevnov, Troja, Nusle, Vršovice, Zbraslav (the whole first tier, 19 quarters); second tier batch 1: Braník, Krč, Podolí, Michle, Kunratice, Záběhlice, Hlubočepy, Košíře; batch 2: Motol, Jinonice, Liboc, Ruzyně, Střešovice, Vokovice, Sedlec, Suchdol; batch 3: Bohnice, Kobylisy, Vysočany, Prosek, Hloubětín, Strašnice, Malešice, Hostivař; batch 4: Černý Most, Dolní Počernice, Kyje, Chodov, Háje, Modřany, Stodůlky, Řeporyje; batch 5: Radotín, Velká and Malá Chuchle, Řepy, Letňany, Čakovice, Kbely, Horní Počernice, Klánovice, Uhříněves (the whole second tier, 42 quarters, 61 guides in all); then the third tier, the remaining 51 small villages in two or three short sections each (overview, history, a fun fact where there is one, landmarks where cards exist), so every one of the 112 quarters has a guide (2026-11-04). Every second-tier quarter got a one-line blurb in `areaBlurbs.js`; the More about this area link shows even where no blurb exists yet. **Quarter menu (2026-11-04, user's call):** the historical-division dropdown lists only about 30 best-known quarters (`FEATURED_QUARTERS` in `AreaFilter.jsx`) plus "Show all ›", which opens `QuarterPickerModal` with all 112 quarters grouped by district; the grouping is the static `utils/quarterDistricts.js` (each quarter under the district holding most of its cards, the nine card-less ones placed by hand; a test checks it covers every quarter). `AREA_GUIDE_KEYS` in `utils/areaGuides/index.js` lists the areas with a guide and must match the data files (`areaGuides.test.js` also checks the three languages have the same sections and paragraphs).
