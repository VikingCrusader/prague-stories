# Prague Stories: The Player's Handbook

> *You have entered the city. The cobblestones are real. The XP is also real. Proceed.*

---

## So You've Opened a Map App That Calls Itself an RPG

Correct. Prague Stories is what happens when someone looks at a beautiful medieval city and thinks: *this would be better with a level-up sound effect.*

The premise is simple. Prague and the rest of Bohemia are full of real places waiting to be found: castles, cemeteries, dynamite factories, a psychiatric hospital that hosts a music festival, a river confluence that almost nobody visits. You go there. You stand there. The app notices you standing there. You get XP. Repeat until you are the most powerful tourist in Bohemia.

There are no orcs. There is, however, a bridge that has had more people thrown off it than any reasonable bridge should. That counts.

Every location comes with its own story: part history, part legend, told with more enthusiasm than strictly necessary. The loop is explore, collect, read, find somewhere new, repeat. A virtuous cycle built on cobblestones, tram tickets and a slowly growing affection for a city that has survived a remarkable amount.

## Before You Leave the House

### Step 1: Create Your Character

Hit **Register**. Choose a username you will not regret in six months. Enter your email and a password.

You begin at **Level 1: Newcomer**. This is accurate. Embrace it.

(You can also browse as a guest. Guests can read, look at the map and admire the pixel art, but collecting needs an account. XP has to be stored somewhere.)

### Step 2: Install as an App (Recommended)

Prague Stories runs full-screen on your phone, with no browser bar and no pasting the link every time.

**iPhone (Safari):**
1. Open Safari. It has to be Safari, not Chrome or any other browser.
2. Go to the app URL.
3. Tap the **Share** button (the box with an arrow pointing up) at the bottom of the screen.
4. Scroll down and tap **Add to Home Screen**.
5. Tap **Add**. The icon appears on your home screen.

**Android (Chrome):**
1. Open Chrome and go to the app URL.
2. Tap the **three-dot menu (⋮)** in the top-right corner.
3. Tap **Add to Home Screen** or **Install App**.
4. Tap **Add**. The icon appears on your home screen.

> Once installed, it opens full-screen like a native app, with no address bar and no browser UI. Tap the icon and you're in Prague.

### Step 3: Allow Location Access

The app will ask to use your location. Say yes. This is how it knows you are actually standing on Charles Bridge and not lying on your sofa in a different country claiming to have visited it. The system has seen this before. The system is not impressed.

> **Note:** location access is required for collecting. Deny it and you can still admire the map very nicely, while earning precisely zero XP. A beautiful, XP-free experience.

### Step 4: Allow Notifications (Optional, Recommended)

The app will also offer location alerts. Say yes to those too. They're how the app taps you on the shoulder when you walk past something you haven't collected yet (more on that below).

### Step 5: Install Google Maps

Not strictly part of this app, but you'll want it. Every location, locked or not, can hand you straight over to Google Maps for directions. With the app installed on your phone, the directions open there instead of in a browser tab.

## The Screens

### Explore

Every location in the game, as a wall of cards sorted by distance, so the nearest mystery is always first. Filter by label or rarity, search by name (which spoils some of the mystery, but is allowed), or switch to **My Collections** to admire past conquests. Inside My Collections you can also sort by when you collected each card, for a smug little trip down memory lane.

Labels are granular and stackable: filter by Church, by Castle, or by both at once if you're feeling specific.

Cards you haven't collected yet are grey, padlocked, and show a coy **???** where the name should be. Cards you collected in the last 48 hours wear a little green **NEW** tag, so you can admire them a bit longer.

### The Map

Your command centre. Gold markers are yours, grey ones are still up for grabs, and the red dot is you. Tap a marker to see what's there.

While the app is open, it quietly keeps track of where you are. That's what powers the 50-metre alerts described below.

### Location Detail

Tap any card to open its full entry. This is where the lore lives:

- **Cover image**: pixel art of the location, in colour once it's yours
- **Rarity and XP**: colour-coded, because the game wants you to feel things about a gold card
- **Labels**: what kind of place this is
- **The story**: in English, Czech or Chinese. Switch mid-read; the lore holds up in all three
- **Wikipedia link**: for when you want the non-RPG version of the same facts
- **Google Maps directions**: "Navigate to: Bohnice Psychiatric Hospital" is a sentence your phone will say without irony
- **The Collect button**: gold, prominent, beckoning (only on cards you haven't collected yet)

### History Timeline

The **History** page tells Bohemia's story from the founding legends onwards, one event at a time, grouped into eras, with illustrations and the occasional quote from a medieval chronicler who clearly had opinions.

Many events end with a row of location cards: the real places where it all happened. Tap one and its location opens right there on top of the page, without losing your place. Reading about a defenestration is one thing; standing under the actual window is better, and it pays XP.

The page remembers where you stopped reading (across devices, if you're signed in), so you can pick up the Middle Ages exactly where you left them.

### The Blind Draw

For everyone who stares at the Explore grid for eleven minutes and still can't pick a location. Once every 24 hours, hit **Draw** and the app deals you one random card from everywhere you haven't found yet. Face down. No peeking, no re-rolls.

What it's worth is explained in the XP section. Spoiler: a lot.

### The Dashboard

Your character sheet:

- **Level badge**: your current level and title, displayed prominently so you can feel things about it
- **XP bar**: a satisfying gold fill creeping towards the next level. Very Skinner box. Very effective.
- **Progress ring**: what percentage of the map you have conquered, expressed as a circle
- **Category breakdown**: how many places you've collected of each kind. Useful for noticing that you have visited seventeen churches and zero places to eat, and should perhaps have lunch
- **Achievements**: see below
- **Level roadmap**: every level at once, past ones glowing gold, future ones grey and taunting

## How Collecting Works

Collecting a location means turning up there in person. The app has several ways of noticing that you have, depending on how close you get.

### Within 100 m: the lock starts to shake

On the Explore page, a locked card within 100 metres of you suddenly changes. Its name appears. Its colours come back (all except the cover, which stays grey until it's yours). And its padlock starts to tremble, like a parcel that knows it's about to be opened.

Think of it as the card noticing you before you notice it.

### Within 100 m: tap the shaking card

Tap a trembling card and its location opens and collects itself on the spot. No buttons to hunt for, no forms. Just a card that has been waiting for you.

### Within 50 m: the alert

Walk within 50 metres of anything you haven't collected and your phone gets a notification, even if you're in another app. (On Android this works with the screen off too; on iPhone, keep Prague Stories open in the background.)

Tap the notification and the app opens the location and collects it for you. No further action required.

On a computer, the alert appears as a banner inside the app instead, and its **Collect** button does the same thing.

Each place alerts you only once per session, so the app won't nag you about the same church every time your GPS twitches.

### Anywhere within 100 m: the Collect button

The classic. Open the location yourself and tap **Collect**. Old-fashioned, reliable, always works.

### What happens next

Whichever way you choose, the same thing happens behind the scenes:

1. The app quietly asks your phone for your GPS position.
2. The server checks whether you are really within 100 metres of the location.
3. If you aren't: you get an error and the strong implication that you are not where you said you were.
4. If you are: the card flips open, the XP lands, and any achievements (or a level-up) pop up.
5. The location stays open so you can start reading the newly unlocked story straight away. Close it whenever you're done.

**Collections are permanent.** There is no undo button. You were there, and the archive remembers.

## Locked and Unlocked

### Locked: a taste, and a signpost

A locked location gives you the opening paragraph of its story and not a word more. After that, the archive politely declines to say anything further until you show up in person. It is less a spoiler policy and more a very effective extortion mechanism.

The big padlock on a locked location's cover isn't just decoration. **Tap it and Google Maps opens with directions straight to the place.** Make sure Google Maps is installed on your phone, or the shortcut ends up in a browser tab instead of the app.

### Unlocked: the whole story

Collect a location and everything opens up: the full history, plus the **bonus paragraph** at the end, the little extra the archive was saving for people who actually came. The cover gets its colours back, and tapping it still gets you directions, in case you want to bring a friend next time.

## The Unlock Show

Every collection comes with a ceremony. The padlock falls away, an open one takes its place, and the card flips over with a flash of light and a sound effect to match. Epic, Mythic and Legendary cards get a grander fanfare.

After that, the card wears its rank for good, everywhere it appears. The rarer the card, the more it shows off, and every tier keeps the decoration of the tiers below it:

| Rarity | What the collected card does |
|---|---|
| ● **Common** | A clean border. Dignified. Understated. Prague-ordinary. |
| ▲ **Rare** | A thin inner line, like a picture frame. |
| ◆ **Superior** | A double inner line with little L-shaped corner pieces. |
| ★ **Epic** | All of the above, plus a soft band of purple light drifting slowly across the card. |
| ✶ **Mythic** | A crimson rim that slowly breathes, a drifting red glow, and a few faint embers rising from the bottom. |
| ♛︎ **Legendary** | A gold frame with a highlight circling it, a golden shine sweeping up and down, and gold embers rising through the card. Subtle it is not. |

Locked cards show none of this. You have to earn the sparkle.

## XP and Levels

XP accumulates. Levels unlock. Titles change.

Each location has a **rarity**, a card-style tier that tells you how rare a find it is. Rarity sets the XP payout:

| Rarity | XP | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| ● **Common** | +10 | Not ordinary, just Prague-ordinary. Any other city would put these on a postcard. |
| ▲ **Rare** | +20 | Worth seeking out. Smart tourists find them; first-timers mostly walk past. |
| ◆ **Superior** | +30 | A step above rare. Not on the standard tourist radar, but rewarding for anyone willing to look just left of the obvious. |
| ★ **Epic** | +50 | Hidden gems that locals know and tourists don't. Requires effort, curiosity and occasionally two tram transfers. |
| ✶ **Mythic** | +70 | Hard to find, rarely visited, yet historically or culturally significant. You will not stumble upon these. |
| ♛︎ **Legendary** | +100 | The most magnificent landmarks of Prague and Bohemia. If you haven't been here, you haven't really been here. |

Rarity shows up everywhere: the symbol and colour on every card, the card border, the location name. Everything is colour-coded. The game wants you to feel things about a gold card.

### Triple XP: the Blind Draw

Your daily draw stays face down until you go and get it. **Collect it within 24 hours and that one visit pays triple XP.** A Legendary card drawn this way is worth 300 XP instead of 100.

It doesn't matter how you collect it (shaking card, alert or Collect button); the bonus applies either way, once per draw.

Let the clock run out and the card quietly flips back to **???**. No penalty, no XP lost, just a fresh mystery waiting for your next draw. This is not a strategy. This is letting a random-number generator make your evening plans. No judgment; the XP is real either way.

## Achievements

Fifteen of them, unlocked automatically when you hit certain milestones. You do not need to track them; they find you.

They live on the Dashboard. Locked ones are greyed out, because the game wants you to know they exist while being deliberately vague about what triggers a few of them. This is called *mystery*, and it is a deliberate design choice, not laziness.

## Adding Your Own Locations

The **+ Add Location** button lives on the Explore page.

Found somewhere that isn't on the map yet? A hidden courtyard, a peculiar statue, a suspiciously good kebab place? Add it. It appears in your grid straight away, and its story gets written the first time someone opens it.

Custom locations work exactly like the built-in ones: same locks, same shaking, same collecting. The game does not discriminate between a medieval castle and a bench you like.

## Tips From Adventurers Who Have Gone Before

- **Let the cards come to you.** With alerts on, you can simply wander and let your phone buzz when you pass something. Some of the best finds are the ones you weren't looking for.

- **No plan for the afternoon?** Explore is sorted by distance, so the first lock in the grid is the nearest one. Open it, tap the padlock, and let Google Maps do the rest.

- **Keep the app open on the tram.** Watching your red dot drift towards a grey marker is, objectively, the correct way to experience Prague.

- **Mythic and Legendary pay the most.** Open the Rarity filter, pick those tiers, and go there first. The XP is proportional to the effort, and the effort is proportional to how few other tourists are present.

- **Read the History Timeline before a day trip.** Every place is twice as good when you know who was thrown out of which window there.

- **Can't decide? Let the Blind Draw decide.** Whatever it hands you pays triple XP if you collect it within 24 hours: the game's polite way of rewarding decisiveness over analysis paralysis.

- **The Dashboard's category breakdown is a conscience.** If every entry is a church, the game is gently suggesting lunch.

- **Pronunciation is its own achievement.** Saying "Žižkovský televizní vysílač" correctly on the first try is unofficial, unrewarded and deeply satisfying.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Can I collect from home?**
No. The server knows. The server is always watching. The server cares about geographic integrity more than you might expect from a hobby project.

**What if my GPS is inaccurate?**
You need to be within 100 metres, which is generous enough for normal GPS drift and not generous enough for "I am in a different neighbourhood." If it fails, step closer, or give your phone a few seconds to work out where you really are.

**Why didn't I get an alert?**
Alerts need notification permission, and each place only alerts you once per session. Missed it? If the card's padlock is shaking, just tap the card.

**Can I undo a collection?**
No. Collections are permanent. You were there.

**Is there an end?**
100% completion: a progress ring filled entirely with gold. Whether that is an ending or a beginning is a philosophical question the app declines to answer.

**What language should I use?**
All three work. EN for convenience, CZ for immersion, ZH for the particular delight of reading about a psychiatric hospital's music festival in Chinese.
